What I do

I'm a full-stack developer and designer based on building complete products — not just screens or endpoints. Most projects start on a whiteboard, move through Figma, and end up as a Django or Flutter app running in production. I care about the parts users never see: load times, clean data models, and forms that actually work.

Lately I've been building learning platforms, service-delivery apps, and the kind of small business tools that save someone an afternoon of spreadsheet work every week.
